### Caveats / known gaps

- Repeater offsets: the RepeaterBook list view shows only the +/- sign, so the magnitude is the band-standard value (0.6 / 1.6 / 5 / 25 MHz). Check it before transmitting.
- Trunked / digital (P25, DMR, MARCS-IP) entries are flagged `"digital": true` and are NOT offered as tunable. They need trunk-tracking, which rtl_fm can't do.
- CTCSS tones are informational. rtl_fm does not decode tones.
